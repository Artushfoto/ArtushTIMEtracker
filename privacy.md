# Privacy Policy — Artush Time Tracker

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Developer Contact:** [support@artushfoto.eu](mailto:support@artushfoto.eu) | [artushfoto.eu](https://artushfoto.eu)

---

### 1. Overview & Privacy Philosophy
**Artush Time Tracker** is designed with a strict **Privacy-First (Zero-Cloud)** philosophy. We believe that your working habits, client names, billing rates, project earnings, and task logs are confidential and belong solely to you.

Artush Time Tracker is a **standalone desktop application** running locally on your computer. **We do not collect, transmit, sell, or analyze your personal or business data.**

---

### 2. Local Data Storage & Zero Telemetry
* **100% Local Storage:** All logged hours, clients, hourly rates, tags, and notes are stored strictly in a local SQLite database file (`tracker_data.db`) located in your user profile directory.
* **No Telemetry or Analytics:** The application contains zero tracking pixels, analytics cookies, behavioral beacons, or advertising SDKs.
* **No User Accounts:** You do not need to register, provide an email address, or create a password to use Artush Time Tracker.

---

### 3. Network Usage & Permissions
Artush Time Tracker works seamlessly without an internet connection. Network activity is limited strictly to user-initiated actions:
1. **Public Holiday Downloads:** When you choose to download national holiday calendars, the app connects to open holiday endpoints.
2. **PRO License Verification:** When activating a perpetual PRO license, the app securely verifies your license key with the payment processor ([Polar.sh](https://polar.sh)).
3. **External Cloud & NAS Mirroring:** When you enable automatic backup mirroring, the software copies backup ZIP archives to a directory chosen by you (e.g., your local OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or local network NAS path). These files are managed entirely by your local sync client — no data passes through our servers.

---

### 4. GDPR & Data Subject Rights
Because the developer does not process, store, or receive your personal data on remote servers:
* **Access & Portability:** You can export your data at any time in standard formats (PDF timesheets, Excel `.xlsx` spreadsheets, CSV files, and full ZIP database backups).
* **Right to Erasure:** You can delete all your records instantly by deleting the database file or uninstalling the application.
